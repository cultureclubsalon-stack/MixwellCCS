Before you start

  1. Use the color bar iPad, with this page open in the Bluefy app.
  2. Close the Vish app all the way (swipe it up and away). The scale only talks to one app at a time.
  3. Turn the scale on. Leave it unplugged from the charger.
  4. Tap Connect to scale and pick the scale from the list.
